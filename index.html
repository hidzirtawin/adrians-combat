COMBAT DOCTRINE

Chain kills to build a combo multiplier up to x8. Your shield regenerates after a few seconds out of fire, but damage to your LIFE bar is permanent — grab repair cells and shield cores. Watch the LIFE bar at the bottom-left: green is healthy, amber is damaged, red is critical. A red arrow around the crosshair shows where damage came from.

Hellwasps orbit and snipe. Grave Saucers strafe in spreads. Gravewalkers rush, Gorehulks soak damage, Void Spawn arrive in pairs, Ripclaws sprint, Cacklers swarm, Cinderbread hurl gumdrops, Straw Reapers and Frost Maws hunt, Shriek Wraiths wail, Tomb Bound advance, Hell Lanterns float, and Scream Spires flood the room with sound. Rotor Reavers and Sky Shredders own the air, Starfangs and Black Omens bring starship fire, Siege Tanks, Iron Carriers and Grave Artillery roll in, and Pale Phantoms, Hex Casters and Rime Terrors close in. Every third wave can roll an Armoured, Air, Horde or Elite modifier. Void Larvae, Hellbrutes and Iron Reapers bring the heavy fire. Power-ups magnetize to you.

Keep turning — the invasion surrounds you. Open the ARMORY before a run to choose from six primaries and three bombs. Pick Easy / Normal / Hard / Insane on the main menu; it changes enemy health, damage, horde size and score. Use FIRE and RELOAD. Tune the camera FOV, torch, haptics and 3D audio in Settings.

ELEVEN BOSSES

Every fifth wave sends a boss. The first five are The Jailer, Bone Maw, The Grinning One, Void Colossus and The Butcher King; later waves add The Rainmaker, Aureole, The Brood Queen, The Mirrored, Umbra and The Collector. Each attacks differently.

Camera and motion power the AR view and your aim. Allow camera, motion and location when asked. Location keeps enemies anchored as you walk; motion keeps your aim true.

No keyboard, no controller. Just your phone, your room, and two buttons.
